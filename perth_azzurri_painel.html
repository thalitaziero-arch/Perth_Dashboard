📖 How to read these charts

SHOTS PER MATCH

The total number above each bar is the total shots in that match. The red number inside is how many were on target (forced the goalkeeper to save or resulted in a goal).

DUELS WON %

Each bar shows the total duels in that match ("t") and the % won. A duel is any 1v1 contest — defensive, offensive, or aerial. Winning more than 50% means Perth dominated individual battles.

xG — EXPECTED GOALS

xG measures the quality of chances created, not just the number of shots. A shot from close range in a good position = high xG (~0.7). A long-range speculative shot = low xG (~0.04). If Perth score more than their xG, they finished above expectation — and vice versa.

PASS ACCURACY %

The percentage of passes that reached a teammate. Higher is better, but context matters — teams playing shorter passes tend to have higher accuracy. Perth's league average is around 76%.

HMLD by Player

Comparison vs. Elite Benchmarks (by Position)

Team average per position group, latest session, vs. elite reference ranges. Goalkeepers excluded.

Why compare by position? Each role has different physical demands, so players are measured against their own position group and elite benchmarks — not against each other.

Higher or lower — good or bad? HSR: higher is generally better — it reflects sprint conditioning and matches elite demands. Far below benchmark may signal under-conditioning; consistently far above match benchmark in training can signal good sprint capacity. HMLD: it's a load measure, not a performance score — higher means more physical cost. Useful is tracking the trend: a spike with no recovery raises fatigue/injury risk; too low for too long can mean under-training.
